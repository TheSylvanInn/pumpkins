Preserving Brevard's History

Join our community Halloween celebration! The pumpkins will be beautifully displayed along the historic wall on Main Street and Franklin Street at The Sylvan Inn.

It’s $1 to vote, and voting is unlimited. Send everyone you know to vote for your pumpkin! All proceeds support both the Sylvan Inn and Friends of Silvermont restoration efforts.

In Partnership With

Friends of Silvermont

Working together to preserve and restore Brevard's historic landmarks.

Important Dates

  • Registration: Register by Oct 23rd for a FREE spot. Late registration is $10.
  • Pumpkin Drop-off: Oct 29th after 9am. Bring to the receiving table in the front yard.
  • Display & Voting: Oct 29th - 31st (3 Nights) along the historic wall.
